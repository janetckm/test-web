About This Chapter

"All One Needs to Know About AI" is a comprehensive course designed to demystify artificial intelligence (AI) and equip you with the knowledge and skills to leverage AI in your personal and professional life. Curated by Professor Pan Hui and a team of esteemed researchers at HKUST and HKUST(GZ), this course is tailored for individuals from all backgrounds who wish to understand AI's potential and ethical implications. Through engaging lectures, hands-on projects, and critical discussions, you'll gain practical insights into AI literacy, AI-assisted coding, and the ethical considerations crucial for responsible AI use.

By the end of this course, participants will have a well-rounded understanding of AI's capabilities and limitations, be able to apply AI tools in various contexts, and critically assess the ethical implications of AI technologies. Each part culminates in a hands-on project, ensuring that learners not only gain theoretical knowledge but also practical experience to tackle real-world challenges.
